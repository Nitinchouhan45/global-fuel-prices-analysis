Story Architecture & Chapter Breakdown

The accompanying Tableau Story organizes complex econometric data into a logical 4-stage narrative progression:

Chapter 1
Macro Benchmark: Brent Crude Volatility

Establishes international benchmark pricing fluctuations (Brent Crude USD) over time, setting the baseline global cost of unrefined petroleum feedstocks.

Chapter 2
The Taxation Spectrum & Fiscal Policy

Explores Tax Percentage variations worldwide, distinguishing nations that utilize fuel levies as sovereign revenue sources from those insulating domestic consumers.

Chapter 3
Cross-Fuel Spreads: Petrol vs Diesel vs LPG

Compares relative price distributions across fuel types (Petrol USD Liter, Diesel USD Liter, LPG USD Liter), noting implications for public transport and logistics.

Chapter 4
Economic Equity & Subsidy Interventions

Analyzes the interplay between World Bank Income Level and national Subsidy Level, examining how emerging economies shield populations from energy price shocks.
