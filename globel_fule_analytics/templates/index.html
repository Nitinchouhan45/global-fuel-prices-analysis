Research Objectives

Why Global Fuel Analytics Matters

Energy pricing directly dictates inflation, consumer purchasing power, and trade balances. This project systematically dissects how retail pump prices diverge from wholesale crude markets due to fiscal policy interventions.

1
Crude Oil Elasticity Transmission

Measure how rapidly and symmetrically fluctuations in Brent crude per barrel pass through to end-consumer retail fuel prices.

2
Cross-National Fiscal Policy Divergence

Distinguish between nations that treat fuel as a vital fiscal revenue generator versus those that subsidize consumer retail costs.

3
Income Bracket & Regional Clustering

Correlate price burdens across World Bank income levels (High, Upper-Middle, Lower-Middle, Low) and geographical continental blocs.
